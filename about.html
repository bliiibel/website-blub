What About Me?

Hello! I'm Isabel Gia Han

Welcome to my portfolio! I am a Creative Technology student at the TU Twente and Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. I havea love for creating cute and functional digital experiences. With a background in both innovative design and engineering, I bring a unique perspective to my work, blending creativity with technical hardware skills.

My Background

As a 2nd generartion Vietnamese women in the Netherlands I grew up with two distinct different cultures. Where two sides seem to clash, collide but also fuse, I strive for my work to reflect me as an individual but also resonate within people. Growing up I learned to balance individualism and collectivism, designing my own line between Western and South East Asia and creating my own space between.
