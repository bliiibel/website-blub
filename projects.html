Project: Aquarium

Date:2026 · Faye & Isabel end assignment

We were inspired by Frutiger Aero, so we made a minimalistic aquarium for a zen vibe, sort of like the relaxing feeling of playing the offline no-wifi game. For our final assignment, we created a peaceful but lively underwater world that you can actively explore. Before diving in, the experience begins with a playful start screen where you can choose to play as a squid, a turtle, a seal, or a seahorse. Once you enter the aquarium, you are greeted by a soothing environment featuring gently moving sand at the bottom and natural, swaying corals. The coding topics covered include object-oriented programming, event handling, vectors, flocking and collision behavior, mass-spring systems, and more.

Project: Tune It Out

Date: 2026

A misophonia awareness game made in Processing. The game is based on reserach done on the medical condition misophonia. The triggers are based on real research and experiences of people. Game Play: You are a student with headphones on, trying to finish an essay while trigger sounds keep coming. Every time, your character freezes and your body reacts. Click the game and type the text on the screen. TAB mutes the sound. Content note: loud sounds and flashing.
