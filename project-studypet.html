StudyPet MEOW

Date: November 2025

StudyPet MEOW is designed to encourage focus. It reacts when you are paying attention and rewards consistent study streaks. The project combines game-like feedback with productivity support.

What I built

  • Focus detection and proximity interaction
  • Progress tracking with streak mechanics
  • Client-server communication
  • Processing and Arduino IDE integration

Skills gained

Interactive design, client-server networking, behavioral gamification, and hardware integration.
